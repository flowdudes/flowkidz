What does it teach?

Color Chaos teaches color theory: warm and cool colors, mixing and contrast, so kids can pick colors that look good together.

How long is it?

Color Chaos takes about 3 minutes. It plays in the web browser with nothing to download, so it fits into a short break.

Is it free?

Yes. Color Chaos is free to play, with no ads, no account and no download.
