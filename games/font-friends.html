What does it teach?

Font Friends teaches font pairing: which fonts work together and why a page needs a clear headline font and an easy-to-read body font.

How long is it?

Font Friends takes about 4 minutes. It plays in the web browser with nothing to download, so it fits into a short break.

Is it free?

Yes. Font Friends is free to play, with no ads, no account and no download.
