The boring but important page

Privacy, in plain words

Flowkidz is made for kids, so we keep this simple. We don't collect personal information. No accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies.

TL;DR: we don't know who you are, and we like it that way.
01

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. We never ask for a name, email, age, photo or location, and there are no accounts to sign up for.

02

What stays on your device

Your browser remembers a few small things in local storage so the site works: which games you played, which games you finished, and which upcoming games you voted for. This data never leaves your device. Clear your browser data and it is gone.

03

How votes work

When you vote for an upcoming game, we add one to that game's vote count. We store the number, not who voted. Nobody, including us, can see which games you picked.

04

The games

Every game runs in your web browser and loads from our own game server. Games have no chat, no ads, no sign-in and no links to social media.

05

Services we use

The site, the games and the vote counts run on Cloudflare. Game pictures load from Webflow's image CDN, and one filter script on the Pick a game page loads from the jsDelivr CDN. Like any web host, these services may keep short-term technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep things secure and working. We don't use analytics cookies or ad trackers.

06

For parents and teachers

Kids can play every game without giving us anything. If we ever change how this works, we will update this page first and change the date below.
